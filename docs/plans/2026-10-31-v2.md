# Convenience Basket v2 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use the host's available task-by-task implementation workflow. Steps use checkbox (`- [x]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add budget adjustment, two-store purchase plans, explicit substitutes, and promotional quantity suggestions while preserving v1.

**Architecture:** Reuse v1 product identities, observations, and `evaluate_line`. Pure services generate reviewable suggestions; Streamlit callbacks apply only the user's selected plan and clear stale comparisons. Keep `main` and `v1-stable` on the original commit; develop in a separate local clone on `v2`.

**Tech Stack:** Python, Streamlit 1.65, pandas, unittest/AppTest, GitHub connector.

## Global Constraints

- Four requested features, version branches, and an independently runnable previous version.
- No public deployment. Keep the week-9 app and original final-project folder unchanged.
- Recommendations use confirmed price/promotion and fresh exact stock only. Missing, stale, conflicting, CACHE, PARTIAL, and ERROR observations must not become confirmed inventory.
- At most five items, 1–10 units each, ten nearby stores, at most two stores per split plan. Never split one item's quantity between stores. Distances are straight-line estimates, not walking times.
- Locked items retain their complete requested quantity in budget plans. Other items may decrease to zero. Rank by fewest units removed, then fewest changed items, then price and deterministic store order. Never suggest an empty basket.
- Substitutes require explicit user-selected candidates and volume/category restrictions. No name-only automatic substitution. Show evidence and request a click before applying.
- Quantity suggestions keep the requested quantity as baseline and suggest at most the rest of a 1+1/2+1 bundle within confirmed stock and the ten-unit limit. Display extra cash cost explicitly.
